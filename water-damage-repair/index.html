Maintenance Tips for Water Damage Repair

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • In case the insurance company asks down the road, photograph anything damaged while it is still in place.
  • Keep leftover flooring, tile and paint details so future repairs can be matched.
  • Hold off on heavy traffic and wet cleaning until new adhesives, caulk and paint have cured.
  • Do not paint or patch over a water stain until the source is fixed.
  • Store pictures of the finished work alongside the scope for future reference.
  • Once a year, renew the caulk at sinks, showers and tubs to keep water from entering the wall.

Signs It Is Time for Water Damage Repair

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Seams are opening, or edges are curling and swelling, on vinyl plank or laminate.
  • Walking over the formerly wet area, you feel soft spots in the floor.
  • A door frame or slab swelled, so the door rubs or fails to close.
  • After drying, the hardwood is still cupped, crowned or lifting.
  • Trim around doors and along the floor is bowed and will not lie flat.
  • Water under the tile left cracked grout lines or tiles that rock when stepped on.
  • Following water from above, part of the ceiling droops, has cracks or crumbles when touched.

What a Professional Brings to Water Damage Repair

Repair after water damage is not ordinary remodeling. Whoever rebuilds has to understand where the water travelled, what it affected and whether the framing inside the wall has fully dried. Because our crew has the drying-phase moisture logs, it knows where studs were wet, what subfloor received treatment and which areas deserve another check. That history keeps problems from being covered over with new drywall and fresh paint.

Then there is the craft of making things match. A patch that shows is a repair you notice every day.
