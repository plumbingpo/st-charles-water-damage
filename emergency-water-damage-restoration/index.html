How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Water has reached the electrical panel, appliances or power strips on the floor.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • You came home to water and do not know how long it ran.
  • Pooled water is creeping over the floor into neighboring rooms or onto the staircase.
  • Part of your ceiling bows downward because water is pooling on top of it.
  • The tank on your water heater gave out and its contents are spreading through the room.

Emergency Water Damage Restoration Prevention Tips

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Shut off the water at the main whenever you leave home for more than a few days.
  • Important paperwork and valuables belong above floor level, most of all on the lowest story.
  • Place leak sensors near the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and under sinks.
  • Pipes running through unheated areas need insulation to keep them from freezing and bursting.
  • Save an emergency restoration company's number in your contacts ahead of time.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.
